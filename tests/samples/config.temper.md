# kelpto-sample-tests

Worked examples of walking and transforming whole documents with
kelpto-md, run on real ones: the CommonMark spec and READMEs. A library
of its own, apart from Kelpto's code, since the spec is under CC-BY-SA
4.0 (see `README.md` here and `NOTICE` at the top of the repo). Nothing
depends on it, and it isn't published.
