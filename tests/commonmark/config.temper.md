# kelpto-commonmark-tests

Tests of kelpto-md against the CommonMark spec's examples. A library of
its own, apart from Kelpto's code, since the examples come from the
spec, under CC-BY-SA 4.0 (see `README.md` here and `NOTICE` at the top
of the repo). Nothing depends on it, and it isn't published.
