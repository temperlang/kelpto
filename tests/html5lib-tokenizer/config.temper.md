# kelpto-html5lib-tokenizer-tests

Tests of kelpto-html's tokenizer against html5lib-tests' tokenizer
tests. A library of its own, apart from Kelpto's code, as the other
vendored tests are (see `README.md` here and `NOTICE` at the top of the
repo). Nothing depends on it, and it isn't published.
