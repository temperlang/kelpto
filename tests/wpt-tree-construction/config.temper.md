# kelpto-wpt-tree-construction-tests

Tests of kelpto-html's tree construction against web-platform-tests'
tree construction tests. A library of its own, apart from Kelpto's
code, as the other vendored tests are (see `README.md` here and
`NOTICE` at the top of the repo). Nothing depends on it, and it isn't
published.
