# kelpto-gfm-tests

Tests of kelpto-md against GitHub Flavored Markdown's examples. A
library of its own, apart from Kelpto's code, since the examples come
from cmark-gfm's spec and tests, under CC-BY-SA 4.0 (see `README.md`
here and `NOTICE` at the top of the repo). Nothing depends on it, and
it isn't published.
