# GFM examples

Generated by `scripts/gfm_spec.py` from cmark-gfm's test/spec.txt (the
GFM spec), test/extensions.txt, and test/regression.txt (its footnote
examples), tag 0.29.0.gfm.13, under CC-BY-SA 4.0 (see NOTICE at the top of
the repo). Don't edit by hand.

Each example: where it's from (`spec`, `extensions`, or
`regression`), its number there, its section, the extensions it needs
(`table`, `strikethrough`, `autolink`, `tagfilter`, `footnotes`, or
`disabled`, space-separated; empty for plain CommonMark, and for
every one from extensions.txt, which runs with all of them), the
Markdown, and the expected HTML.

backend compiles them easily.
