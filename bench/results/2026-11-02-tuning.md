# kelpto-md tuning, 2026-11-02

kelpto-md parsing before (commit 9c59f20) and after the first tuning,
the two builds timed alternately, three rounds each, on the same
machine as [2026-11-02.md](2026-11-02.md), which was busy with other
builds at the time: absolute times here run 1.5 to 2.5 times that
run's, so compare the two columns, not those times. Each cell is the
median over the rounds of each round's median parse, with the lowest
and highest round in brackets. Not a run of `scripts/bench.py`: the
other libraries weren't timed.

A one-round `scripts/bench.py` run of Rust, earlier and on a quieter
machine, gave 84 ms for the spec (94.4 ms in 2026-11-02.md), 99
times pulldown-cmark's time and 27 times comrak's (104 and 30
before), and every pathological shape at ×1.9 to ×2.1 per doubling,
nested block quotes and lists included (×3.1 to ×3.9 and ×2.3 to
×2.5 before).

Deep nesting: Python parses and prints back 5,000 nested block quotes
(it stopped at 1,000 before); rendering them and `descendants` still
recurse, and hit Python's limit there.
